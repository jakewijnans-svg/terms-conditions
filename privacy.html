Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Money Hustler publishes personal finance education videos. Money Hustler Bot is a private tool that publishes our own videos to our own accounts.

Which data the app uses

Money Hustler Bot signs in only to the channel owner's own Google account, using Google OAuth. With the permissions granted, it:

  • uploads our own videos and thumbnails to our own YouTube channel;
  • reads basic information about that channel (name and ID) to check the connection.

The app does not collect, read or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users. It does not use data for advertising and does not sell or share data with third parties.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared. It can be deleted at any time by removing it from that computer.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google security settings page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions? Contact us through the Money Hustler YouTube channel.
